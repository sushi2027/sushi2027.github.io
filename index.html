Welcome

We are delighted to invite you to the SUSHI Symposium 2027, taking place on 25–26 July 2027 at the University of Glasgow. This meeting will bring together researchers working with human single unit recordings from across the globe, to present their latest research findings and discuss the future direction of the field. The first day of the meeting will focus on the medial temporal lobe, and its role in spatial and episodic memory function; while the second day of the meeting will focus on the role of cortical networks in auditory cognition and language processing.

The symposium takes place immediately before the International Conference on Memory, also hosted by the University of Glasgow.
